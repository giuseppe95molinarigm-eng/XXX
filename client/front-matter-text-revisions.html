Copyright page · page 4

Blueprint

Borders are shown as generally recognized at the time of printing, and the publisher takes no position on territories under dispute. Figures for population and area are approximate and current to 2026.

Printed on FSC-certified 100 gsm cream Biotop, an uncoated paper chosen for its response to pencil.

Revised

Borders follow United Nations practice. Where a dispute affects a country’s figures, a short note in small type on that page explains it. The publisher takes no position on territories under dispute. All figures refer to 2025. Population figures are United Nations estimates for 1 July 2025, rounded (United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division, World Population Prospects 2024, licensed under CC BY 3.0 IGO). Areas are official national figures.

Printed on FSC-certified 100 gsm cream uncoated paper, chosen for its response to pencil. (The paper's name goes in once the printer confirms Biotop or an equivalent.)

The rest of the copyright page is unchanged.

Before You Set Out · pages 9–12

Three paragraphs describe things that have since changed. Everything else in the essay stands.

Paragraph 3 · Blueprint

Each one carries the same things: the country’s name in English and in its own language, its outline, its flag drawn as an open line, a short register of facts, and, at the foot of the page, two spaces that belong entirely to you.

Revised

Each one carries the same things: the country’s name in English and in its own language, its outline, its flag drawn as an open line, a short register of facts, a drawing of what the country is best known for, and, at the foot of the page, two spaces that belong entirely to you.

On coloring · Blueprint

The flags are printed as outlines, and their true colors are listed at the back of the book, in words rather than in numbers, so that nothing interrupts the page. Colored pencil suits this paper best. Biotop is uncoated and slightly warm, and it takes graphite and pencil beautifully…

Revised

The flags are printed as outlines. Beneath each one, a row of small circles names its colors in the order they appear, and at the back of the book A Register of Colors explains, in plain words, which part takes which color. Colored pencil suits this paper best. It is uncoated and slightly warm, and it takes graphite and pencil beautifully…

On the other pages · Blueprint

Near the front there is a map of the world to color as you go, a running list of every country you have visited, and a hundred numbered lines for the places you intend to reach. At the close of each continent there is room for the notes that belong to no single country, which is where the long train journeys and the difficult borders tend to end up. And at the back there is a ledger: your totals, your best meal, the strangers whose names you never learned, the words you brought home from other languages.

Revised

Near the front there is a map of the world to color as you go and a running list of every country you have visited. Each continent closes with fifteen experiences worth the journey, a page to mark what you achieved there, and room for the notes that belong to no single country, which is where the long train journeys and the difficult borders tend to end up. And at the back there is a ledger: your totals, your top fives, your best meal, the strangers whose names you never learned, the words you brought home from other languages, one favorite memory, and the places you mean to go next.
